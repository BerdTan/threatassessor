What the data says

⚡
Tester is the hardest role
The tester critic shows the widest variance of any role — from 0.0 (multiple models) to 11.8 (Qwen3.6 on microservices). Models that over-generate tokens in reasoning mode exhaust their budget before producing valid JSON, collapsing to zero.
🏛
Architecture sensitivity
Qwen3.6's tester scores 2.4 on AI-system archs but 11.8 on microservices — same model, same prompt, 5× difference. Blackhat and architect are far more stable across arch types.
🧩
MiniMax: the consistent free model
MiniMax M3 delivers the most consistent scores across all three deep archs of the free-tier models. Tester (7.0–10.6), blackhat (10.6–11.0), and red_team (9.0–10.4) stay competitive.
🔍
The mystery model reveal
ox-alpha — listed as a stealth preview — was revealed on retirement to be Z.AI's GLM-5.3 Flash. Its blackhat score of 10.9 is the highest single-critic score in the entire dataset.
🚀
Nemotron Nano surprises
Nemotron Nano 30B/3A scores 11.6 architect on GCP Serverless — matching the reference Qwen3.6 model. Tester collapses to 0 (token budget), but blackhat (11.4) and red_team (10.8) are strong across both tested archs.
📐
Blackhat is the great equaliser
Blackhat scores cluster between 9.0–10.9 across all models and archs — the tightest spread of any role. Even models that collapse on tester perform well here, suggesting adversarial framing elicits reliable reasoning across model families.

† GLM-5.3 Flash (ox-alpha): AI Nodes arch only — retired 2026-08-26. Now paid ($0.075/$0.25 per 1M).

‡ Nemotron Nano: AI Nodes + GCP Serverless — purple_team missing on AI Nodes (reasoning blowout). Times out on larger archs at 1500s.

§ Nemotron Super 120B: AI Nodes + GCP Serverless + Microservices (Agentic AI timed out). Architect/tester 0.0 on Microservices — 15–24k token over-generation exhausts output budget.

Scores are depth scores from ThreatAssessor critic rubric (max 12 per critic). Each model ran FULL_MOE pipeline — same harness, same system prompts, same arch inputs.
