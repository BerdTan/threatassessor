Weighting rationale

Tier weights (T1×1.0 → T4×2.5) are a temporary scaffold, not a permanent fixture. They compensate for the fact that the current TATB rubric does not yet differentiate structural complexity — all architectures max at 13 regardless of component count or domain novelty.

The long-term goal is rubric-as-truth: a well-calibrated TATB should make a 13 on T4 intrinsically harder to earn than a 13 on T1, making equal weights fair. As the rubric matures — deeper TB dimension, agentic PA tiers, cross-boundary RD criteria — the tier multipliers should converge toward 1.0.

If the leaderboard still needs external weights to rank tools fairly at that point, it is a signal that the rubric needs to grow further, not that the weights need to increase. DECISIONS Entry 248.
